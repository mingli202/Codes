# Sokoban

# OVERVIEW

Sokoban is a puzzle game. Your goal is to push colored trophies onto their matching colored targets to win the level.

## GAME ELEMENTS

  Player (>)   - You! Shown facing right, left, up, or down.
  Crate  (B)   - An obstacle on the board.
  Wall   (W)   - Impassable barrier.
  Trophy (r/g/b/y) - Colored item (red, green, blue, yellow) to push onto
                     its matching target.
  Target (R/G/B/Y) - Colored goal marker. Match the trophy color to win.
  Blank  (_)   - Empty space.


## HOW TO WIN

Push every colored trophy onto the target of the same color.
The level is complete when all targets are covered by their
matching trophy.
